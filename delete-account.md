# Delete Your Drivably Account

## How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Drivably account directly from within the app:

1. Open the Drivably app
2. Tap the **Account** tab (bottom right)
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account will be permanently deleted immediately.

## What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

- Your account profile and display name
- All community traffic reports you submitted
- Your saved places and routes
- Your karma points and streak history
- Your push notification token

## What Is Not Deleted

- Reports that have already been merged with other reports may remain anonymized in the system
